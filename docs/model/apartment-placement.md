`apartmentToSite` and `siteToApartment` convert positions. `apartmentDirectionToSite` and `siteDirectionToApartment` rotate vectors without applying the floor or site translation. Thus a true-north solar direction can be rendered in either scene without changing the astronomical result. `splitTargetBuildingFootprint` returns the before / apartment band / after polygons in site coordinates, enabling lower and upper building masses plus flanking volumes to retain their shadows while leaving the apartment void.
