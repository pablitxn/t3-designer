# Demonstration model: provenance and visual inventory

The public scene preserves authored apartment geometry, materials, furnishings
and approximate area values. It is a generalized demonstration, not a property
condition report. Its plan is rendered from the model and contains no original
address or diagnosis identifier. Source photos, videos and private documents are
not redistributed in this dossier.

## What can be inspected in the model

| Space | Represented elements | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance | Doors, exposed services, cabinet and localized floor wear | Shapes and damage extent are illustrative. |
| Living room | Parquet, closet, radiator and double balcony door | Dimensions, clearances and material appearance are estimates. |
| Bedrooms | Double windows, shutters and radiators | Window placement and heights are approximate. |
| Kitchen | U-shaped counters, cabinets, sink and appliances | A conceptual layout, without verified product performance. |
| Bathroom | Vanity, washer, shower and glass-block partition | Metric fit remains an authored approximation. |
| WC | Separate toilet and outward-opening door | Clearances and hidden details are not measured. |
| Balcony | Door and railing | Proportions and rail spacing are approximate. |

## Reading the evidence

The local geometry is the source of the public plan. The eight example room
areas total 49.18 m²; walls overlap conceptual zone boundaries, so that sum is
not a measured net-area certificate. Balcony and basement values are separate;
the basement is not reconstructed.

Historical model evidence tags refer to authoring observations, not accessible
private files. Public claims are limited to the authored model and declared
assumptions. No original audiovisual content is needed to build or render it.
Visual wear is representative, not a diagnosis of hidden causes or condition.

The generalized building context retains open-data attribution as described in
the repository building research guide. Third-party materials keep
their own rights; the repository license does not relicense an original document.

To use the method on another property, first authorize its publication scope,
then record each source's issuer/date/access and exact page, field or timestamp.
Keep document observations, transcribed values, calculations and modeling
estimates distinguishable. Review both metadata and visible text before sharing.
